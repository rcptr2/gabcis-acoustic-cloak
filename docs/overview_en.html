GABCI'S ACOUSTIC CLOAK

A phase-rotation sidechain engine for zero-loudness-loss Kick/Bass alignment.

Instead of ducking the bass on every kick hit, Acoustic Cloak rotates the bass's phase within an adjustable frequency range so it sums constructively with the kick — full loudness, no volume loss, no pumping.

VST3 · Standalone macOS & Windows 11 x64 JUCE 9 · C++20 v1.0.0

What Makes It Different

Traditional sidechain compression

Ducks the bass's volume whenever the kick hits — trading loudness for separation. The lower the crossover, the more audible the pumping.

Acoustic Cloak's approach

Measures the actual phase relationship between kick and bass inside a user-defined low-frequency window, then rotates the bass's phase so the two sum constructively — no gain reduction anywhere in the signal path.

Technical Architecture

Three purpose-built DSP stages, each individually measured and verified (not assumed) — see Quality & Performance for the actual numbers.

Solid arrows = audio signal. Dashed arrows = analysis/control data only.

Core Technology

  • BandIsolator — a two-stage 4th-order Linkwitz-Riley crossover that isolates the user's Target Frequency Range with a mathematically flat magnitude sum, measured within 2% across a 10Hz–8kHz sweep and <3% right at the crossover edges.
  • AllpassHilbertNetwork — an 8-stage interleaved IIR allpass Hilbert transformer (coefficients derived directly from the bilinear transform of an analog allpass, not a memorised table), giving a genuine analytic (I/Q) signal with exact magnitude preservation — no resonant filter, no image-leakage artefact.
  • The "Dummy All-Pass" recombination — the residual (everything outside the target band) is warped by an exact duplicate of the same allpass cascade used to rotate the target band. Because filtering is linear, the two paths always recombine flat, at any rotation angle — not just when the effect is off.
  • PhaseCorrelationAnalyzer — a continuous (not snapshot-based) phase-difference tracker with a broadband, non-resonant Transient Sensitivity gate, so short kick transients are never smeared by filter ringing.
  • Holographic Radar display — a real-time polar visualisation of the measured phase relationship, with a phosphor-glow, medical-instrument aesthetic.

Why This Is Unique

Most "phase alignment" plugins either (a) apply a simple fixed delay, which cannot correct a genuine phase relationship across a band, or (b) use a resonant filter shortcut that colours the signal outside its own narrow passband. Acoustic Cloak's design went through three independent engineering-review cycles specifically to eliminate both failure modes:

Design consideredWhy it was rejected
STFT / overlap-add phase vocoderImpractical FFT window size for 20-150Hz resolution; latency and pre-ringing risk
Summed multi-resonator bank (for audio, not just analysis)Overlapping resonant filters produce a "lumpy", non-flat combined magnitude response
Nested crossover sub-bandsMeasured ~20% interference — crossover points too close together in a narrow band
Single resonator + delta correctionMeasured ~65% rotation error from negative-frequency image leakage
